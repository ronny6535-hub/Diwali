      About the event

      One roof.
      One day.
      The whole festival.

      Diwali shopping usually means a run across the suburbs — sweets from one place, gifts from another, an outfit from somewhere else, and the diyas you forgot until the night before. This is all of it in one room, on one Sunday, with parking at the door.

      Now in its third year, the Diwali Exhibition Sale brings independent makers and small local businesses together under one roof at the Campbelltown Function Centre in Rostrevor, Adelaide. Entry is free, the stalls are hand-picked, and there's a mystery gift drawn live before the doors close.
